Never reorder Items_AttachmentPart
----------------------------------
A saved vehicle refers to its parts by position in that table, not by row name.
Move a row and every row below it shifts, and existing builds silently come back
wearing whatever slid into the slot -- a truck that had a sunvisor turns up with
something else on it.

Learned the hard way: six headlights were lifted to index 35 to make them easier
to find in the menu, which pushed 587 rows down by six. Moving them to the *end*
is not a fix either -- they were originally interleaved alphabetically, so
pulling them out of the middle shifts 134 rows just the same. The only safe
order is the one already there.

New rows are fine. run.py appends, and appending changes nobody's index. If a
part needs to be easier to find, that is a vendor or UI problem, not a table
order problem.
