# Company Size Unlimited

## Short description

Up to 100 company vehicles instead of 20, and more of them on the road at once.

## Long description

The game lets a company buy 15 extra vehicle slots on top of its 5, for 20 vehicles in all. Company Size Unlimited raises that to 95 extra slots, so a company can own up to 100 vehicles.

### What you get

- Buy up to 95 vehicle slots (vanilla 15), for up to 100 company vehicles. Each slot costs a little more than the one before, as in vanilla.
- The company screen's counter shows the real number, and every vehicle past 20 is kept when the save loads.
- More company vehicles out at once: up to 100 per company and 500 in all.

### What you need

- Plugin Loader (its own download).

### Install

1. In Steam, right-click Motor Town > Manage > Browse local files.
2. Drag the MotorTown folder from this download into that window and let it merge.

Settings are in MotorTown\Binaries\Win64\plugins\company_size_unlimited.ini, created on first launch: MaxBoughtSlots (95), MaxSpawnedPerCompany (100), MaxSpawnedPerCorporation (100) and MaxSpawnedManagedFleetTotal (500). Disabled=1 under [General] turns the plugin off.

### Multiplayer

Works in single player and on a server you host. On a dedicated server the plugin has to be on the server. A player on a vanilla server keeps the game's 20.
