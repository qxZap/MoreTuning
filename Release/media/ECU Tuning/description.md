# ECU Tuning

## Short description

An ECU Tuning page in the garage, set per vehicle: tune the engine and get the exhaust to match: anti-lag, turbo flutter, pops, bangs and real flames from the exhaust tips.

## Long description

ECU Tuning adds its own section to the garage's Tuning screen. Every vehicle keeps its own tune.

### What you get

- Engine tuning: ignition timing, fuel map and boost pressure, with 0.50 as stock. More power costs heat and fuel, and the tune shapes how the exhaust behaves.
- Cooling boost: up to three times the radiator's cooling.
- Anti-lag on turbo cars: above the rpm you set, the turbo stays spun up for a moment after you lift off. Touching the brake cancels it.
- Turbo flutter when you lift off from high revs.
- Pops and bangs when you lift off the throttle. A rich or retarded map fires more often and for longer.
- Exhaust flames from the car's own exhaust tips, lighting up what is around them. A car with its catalytic converter still fitted stays clean; take it off and the flames come.
- DPF delete on diesels: heavier, longer smoke.
- Supercharger whine on supercharger intakes.

### What you need

- Plugin Loader (its own download).
- Nothing else. This download is a bundle: the plugin plus its own sound pak, with the turbo flutter and the supercharger whine. Install both together.
- The whine plays on supercharger intakes, which come with MoreTuning. Without MoreTuning everything else works the same.

### Install

1. In Steam, right-click Motor Town > Manage > Browse local files.
2. Drag the MotorTown folder from this download into that window and let it merge.

Settings are in MotorTown\Binaries\Win64\plugins\ecu_tuning.ini, created on first launch. Disabled=1 under [General] turns the plugin off.

### Multiplayer

Other players see and hear your pops and bangs. The engine changes only take effect in single player or when you are the host.
