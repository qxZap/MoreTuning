# Suspension Tuning

## Short description

See and tune what sits behind your wheels: brake discs, calipers, coilovers, arms and drive shafts that move with the suspension, paintable, set per axle, with big brake kits to fit.

## Long description

Suspension Tuning puts real brakes and suspension behind your wheels, then lets you paint and tune them.

### What you get

- Brake discs, calipers, wheel spacers, knuckles, coilovers, lower arms and drive shafts at every wheel. They follow the suspension as it moves and spin with the wheel.
- Big brake kits (320, 360 and 400 mm) as Brake Pads parts for any vehicle. A bigger disc heats up less, cools faster, fades later and wears less.
- Eight new entries on the Paint screen: Brake Caliper, Brake Disc, Suspension, Dampers, Wheel Spacer, Lug Nuts, Knuckle & Arms and Drive Shaft.
- A Brakes & Suspension page under Tuning, per axle: brake position, spacer length, mount positions, coilover top and size, and lower arm pivot. Copy and paste a setup between axles.
- Only on your own cars, and off until you switch it on for that car on the Brakes & Suspension page. Never on boats.

### What you need

- Plugin Loader (its own download).
- DataTable Framework, for the big brake kits.
- This download is a bundle: the plugin plus its own pak with the meshes and the brake kits. Install both together.

### Install

1. In Steam, right-click Motor Town > Manage > Browse local files.
2. Drag the MotorTown folder from this download into that window and let it merge.

Settings are in MotorTown\Binaries\Win64\plugins\suspension_tuning.ini, created on first launch. Disabled=1 under [General] turns the plugin off.
