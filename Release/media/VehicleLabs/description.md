# Vehicle Labs

## Short description

Extra rear winches and utility mounts on 34 trucks, fuel pumps on every tanker, and body parts you can take off any vehicle.

## Long description

Vehicle Labs 2.0 for 0.7.20

Ever wanted a second winch? Or a third? Or somewhere to actually put all those utility boxes?
Now you can. Vehicle Labs opens up 34 trucks with extra rear winch points and utility mounts, hand-placed on each vehicle where there's really room for them. Nothing comes pre-fitted: the spots are there, you decide what goes on them.

### Winches and mounts

- The big rigs, 3 rear winches and 10 utility slots: Golima Semi, FL1, Titan, Longhorn Semi 4x2, SRT, Maity, Dumpy, Mixi, Tronko, Atlas 4x2 Semi, Atlas 6x2 Dryvan, Kuda Dryvan, Kuda LiveFishTank. Most of these started with two utility slots and no rear winch at all.
- The working bodies, 3 rear winches and 8 or 9 utility slots: Atlas 6x2 Semi, Atlas 6x4 Semi, Atlas 8x4 Dump, Atlas 6x2 Tanker, Kuda Container, Kuda Flatbed, Kuda Semi.
- The specialists, 2 or 3 rear winches and mounts where the body allows: Terra, Atlas 6x2 Garbage, Compacty, Jemusi, Jemusi Dump, Jemusi Flatbed, Jemusi Tanker.
- Long haulers, 1 rear winch and the full 10 utility slots: Longhorn Semi, Jemusi Semi.
- Kira trucks, 1 rear winch: Kira Van, Kira Box, Kira Flatbed, Kira Rollback, Kira Tanker.

Three rear winches is the ceiling, by the way. That's the game's limit, not mine: there are four winch slots in total and the front winch claims one.

### Fuel pumps on every tanker

Nine tankers can fuel other vehicles straight out of the box: Atlas 6x2 Tanker, Kira Tanker, the 30ft Tanker, the 5t Tanker Trailer, Tanko 40, Taber (lead and rear), Shotan 7 and Shotan 10. They pump diesel, petrol and water, and they fill up with water at a hydrant (the Kira Tanker is fuel only).

### Removable body parts

Front and rear bumpers, bonnet, side skirts, fenders, headlights and roof can be uninstalled on every vehicle, the way a spoiler can.

### Capitalist Economy's vehicle rules, built in

Vehicle Labs carries Capitalist Economy's vehicle changes itself, so the two no longer fight over the same files and no compatibility patch is needed. The bigger dump loads from its Vehicle Cargo Boost are built in on the ten bodies Vehicle Labs ships.

### What changed since 1.0

- Rebuilt for 0.7.20.
- ProxyTech Duallys is no longer required. Because of that, the Hana, Ranch and Voltex no longer get rear winches here.
- Fuel pumps went from two tankers to nine.
- New: removable body parts, and Capitalist Economy's vehicle rules.

### Requirements

- Nothing. DataTable Framework is recommended: with it, Vehicle Labs sits alongside every other vehicle mod instead of replacing their vehicle lists.

### Compatibility

- Capitalist Economy
- Maja's Trailer Works: her meshes show on the Vehicle Labs trucks.
