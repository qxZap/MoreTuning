# Custom Plates

## Short description

Design your own license plates in the garage: your text, your colours, a template to start from. Other players with the plugin see your plate.

## Long description

Custom Plates puts real lettering on the number plate attachments. Fit a plate the usual way with the game's attachment tools, then design it under Tuning > License Plate. Every vehicle keeps its own plate.

### What you get

- Up to nine characters: letters, numbers and a dash.
- Eight templates to start from: European, UK Rear, American, Sunshine, Japanese, Racing, Classic and Prestige. Pick one, then change anything you like.
- Text colour, plate colour, border and a coloured band (left, top or bottom), from a 12-colour palette.
- Text size, height and position on the plate.
- Plate light: off, on with the engine, or always on, in the colour you choose.
- Lettering on both faces of the plate.

### What you need

- Plugin Loader (its own download).
- More Attachments: the plates are its number plate attachments.

### Install

1. In Steam, right-click Motor Town > Manage > Browse local files.
2. Drag the MotorTown folder from this download into that window and let it merge.

Settings are in MotorTown\Binaries\Win64\plugins\custom_plates.ini, created on first launch. Disabled=1 under [General] turns the plugin off.

### Multiplayer

The plate design saves with the car, so other players who have the plugin see your plate. Players without it see the plain plate attachment.
