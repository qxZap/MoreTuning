# Attachment Tweaks

## Short description

Better attachments: copy, paste and mirror a part's placement, control each light on a part on its own, decals on attachments, and attachment light shadows on Cinematic.

## Long description

Attachment Tweaks improves the game's attachment tools. It works on any attachment, the game's own and modded ones.

### What you get

- Clipboard in the placement window: COPY, PASTE and MIRROR under the coordinates. Copy a part's location, rotation and scale, paste them onto another part, or mirror them left to right to place the matching part on the other side. Save as usual to keep it.
- Each light on its own: an attachment with several lights (a light bar, a tail lamp with brake, reverse and signal sections) gets a Toggle, Mode and Color line per section, so each section can be switched, set to its own mode and given its own colour. Modes follow the vehicle: brake, reverse, turn signals, headlights, engine, interior light or siren, or Manual.
- Decals on attachments: the car's decals now show on paintable attachments where they sit on the car, the way they show on its body.
- Light shadows: attachment lights cast shadows only when shadows are set to Cinematic, so a car full of lamps doesn't cost you frames on lower settings.

### What you need

- Plugin Loader (its own download).
- Works with More Attachments: its lamps carry the per-section lines. Neither needs the other. Without the plugin those lines still work, they just switch every section at once.

### Install

1. In Steam, right-click Motor Town > Manage > Browse local files.
2. Drag the MotorTown folder from this download into that window and let it merge.

Light settings are saved per vehicle and part. Settings are in MotorTown\Binaries\Win64\plugins\attachment_tweaks.ini, created on first launch. Disabled=1 under [General] turns the plugin off.

### Multiplayer

The lights work in single player and when you are the host. Decals show only to players who have the plugin.
