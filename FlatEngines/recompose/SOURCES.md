# Recording credits

The engine sounds below are made from these recordings (YouTube; the sound belongs to its uploader). Per rpm
step one steady stretch is used, bent to the step rpm and looped forward then reversed. Steps are the rpm points
in the engine's `MT_<key>_EngineSoundData`. Every download, also the unused ones, is listed in `src/<key>/meta.txt`.
